Data quality

WijnKoop goes through the database once a week and lists what looks wrong: the same wine twice, one wine that is really two, award links to check, colours, prices and producer names. For each one it suggests what to do. You choose, for one at a time or for many at once. What you decide holds when the shops are read again.
