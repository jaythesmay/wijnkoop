How to read a card

The square on the left is the score: all medals and critic points for this wine combined, on a 100-point scale. It turns gold when the wine won a gold medal (or better) or got 93+ points from a critic.

The price is per bottle when you buy 6 of this wine, with the shop's promotion applied.

The label compares the price with what wines with this score usually cost ({{ 'learned from your wines' if rule.learned else 'starting rule' }}: about {{ rule.per_point_pct }}% more per point). Top buy means you pay two thirds or less of the usual price. More about the scores.
