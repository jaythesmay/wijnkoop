Award wines not in your shops

Every award from your uploaded results files is kept, also when none of your shops sells the wine. These are the wines WijnKoop keeps looking for: after every shop run it checks the new wines against this list, and the weekly data quality run checks all wines again. When one turns up in a shop, its award counts and it moves to “Found in a shop” below.
