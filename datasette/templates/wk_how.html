How the scores work

Score

Every medal and critic score for a wine is turned into points on a 100-point scale and averaged, with the big competitions counting most: Decanter, IWC and IWSC count fully, Concours Mondial, Mundus Vini and the well-known critics count 70%, the rest 40%. A medal without points gets the middle of that competition's own band (an IWC gold is 96, a Concours Mondial gold is 90). Bronze and commended medals don't count. A wine with only one small award is pulled a little towards 88, so one lucky medal can't put it at the top.

Gold or better means a gold, grand gold, platinum or trophy from a competition, or 93+ points from a critic.

Price for quality

Better wine costs more, and not in a straight line: every extra point costs a fixed percentage more. WijnKoop works out what a wine with a given score usually costs and compares that with the price you pay per bottle when you buy 6.

Deal

A deal is a price (per bottle at 6, promotion included) at least 15% below this wine's own median price of the last 90 days, or cheaper than in any other shop. Until there are 90 days of prices, the normal shop price is used.
