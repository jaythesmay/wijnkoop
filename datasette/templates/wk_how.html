How the scores work

Score

Every medal and critic score for a wine is turned into points on a 100-point scale and averaged, with the big competitions counting most: Decanter, IWC and IWSC count fully, Concours Mondial, Mundus Vini and the well-known critics count 70%, the rest 40%. A medal without points gets the middle of that competition's own band, because the same word means very different things:

So a gold from a small competition is about as good as a bronze at Decanter or the IWC, and it counts that way. Critics' points count as they are, except Luca Maroni, who scores generously on his own scale up to 99: his points count 6 lower. A wine with only one small award is pulled a little towards 88, so one lucky medal can't put it at the top. On a wine's page, every award shows what it counts as.

Gold or better means an award worth 93 points or more: a gold at Decanter, IWC or IWSC, a grand gold at Mundus Vini or the Concours Mondial, or 93+ from a critic.

Price for quality

Two wines with different scores are made comparable with your own rule: how much more money one extra point is worth to you. Every wine is then shown as “like a 93 for €…”: what it would cost if it were a 93. Lower is better.

Your rule now: {{ rule.rate }}% per point. So a 94 for {{ rule.example|money }} is worth the same to you as a 93 for {{ 10|money }}.

For comparison: in large public sets of critic reviews with prices, one point costs roughly 15 to 20% more between 88 and 95 points. {% if rule.shops_pct %}In your shops right now it is about {{ rule.shops_pct }}% (from {{ rule.fit_wines }} award wines).{% endif %}

Deal

A deal is about the price of this wine: at least 15% below its own median price of the last 90 days (per bottle at 6, promotion included), or cheaper than in any other shop. Until there are 90 days of prices, the normal shop price is used.

Price per bottle

Every price is what one bottle costs when you buy 6 of that wine, with the shop's promotion applied: “2e fles 50% korting” or “2e halve prijs” makes a €10 bottle €7.50, “6 halen 5 betalen” €8.33, “3 voor 2” €6.67. At Gall & Gall the Premium price counts when it is lower (you have Premium); a Premium price and a promotion are not added together.

Prices are compared for a normal bottle of 75 cl. A half bottle of 37.5 cl at €8 counts as €16, a magnum of 1.5 l at €30 as €15. The size comes from the shop, or from the name or address (“37,5cl”, “halve fles”, “Magnum”) when the shop does not say it. Such a wine shows “per 75 cl at 6” with the real bottle size and its price below it; an order of 6 is 6 bottles of the size sold.
